Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the software application, “Music Ball” and was created to inform users about what information is collected if any, why and what is done with it.

Information Collected

Music Ball does not collect or store any information about its users.

Information Shared

We do not share personal information with companies, organizations or individuals unless there is a legal reason to do so. Music Ball is not gathering or storing any user data.

Information Security

Music Ball does not require users to create an account.

Microphone Access

The application requests access to the user’s microphone to be able to play back the user’s own live audio content.

Microphone access is only for audio visualization for purposes within the Music Ball application.

Compliance and Cooperation with Regulatory Authorities

We review our compliance with our Privacy Policy and will work with the appropriate regulatory authorities, including local data protection authorities, to resolve any complaints regarding the transfer of personal data that we cannot resolve with our users directly.

Changes

We will post any privacy policy changes on this page and if the changes are significant, we will provide a more prominent notice.
